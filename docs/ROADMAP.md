# Implementation roadmap

Each phase ends with the same gate:

1. Run the relevant tests (lint, typecheck, unit, DB, E2E as they exist).
2. Verify the working features in a browser (desktop and mobile, English and Arabic).
3. Fix what the checks find.
4. Update the docs (`docs/`, `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`).
5. Summarise completed work and remaining tasks for the owner.
6. Ask for approval before major architectural changes, and before starting the next phase.

## AI sales agent ("GIK Smart Assistant", started 2026-11-09)

The owner asked for a 24/7 AI sales and customer-service chat on every page, in nine steps:
1 inspect, 2 plan and costs, 3 chat window, 4 Claude and knowledge base, 5 lead capture and storage,
6 admin AI Sales dashboard and agent settings, 7 email notifications and WhatsApp preparation,
8 testing, 9 Vercel deployment. Each step needs the owner's approval before the next.

Approved on 2026-11-09 (owner accepted the recommended choices): Claude Sonnet 5.5 for the live chat
(changeable in Settings); contact details collected through a form inside the chat and never sent to
the AI provider; the official Anthropic SDK for streaming replies; a customer-facing AI that answers
directly (a change from "drafts only" in ARCHITECTURE §8, limited by the same hard rules); HOT / WARM /
COLD set by the AI and editable by staff; `/furniture-assistant` kept.

Note: the site is live at https://global-island-kitchens.vercel.app (`/api/health` answered `ok` on
2026-11-09), although the Phase 10 notes below still say nothing was deployed.

## AI Business Automation System (started 2026-11-11)

The owner's brief: four AI agents (sales, marketing and lead generation, social media, business
analytics) sharing the database, a central CRM, a company knowledge base, n8n workflows and a new admin
section, the **AI Business Automation Centre**. Plan approved by the owner on 2026-11-11, built one stage at
a time with approval between stages. Decisions taken: the existing database and native CRM (no Supabase,
no Zoho); the new Centre in English and Arabic, the older admin screens translated later; integrations
without credentials show "Awaiting credentials" and are never shown as connected without a real test.

Open decisions for the owner (asked 2026-11-11): pricing rates for automatic quotation drafts; whether
the forms' consent wording covers marketing; paying per search for Google Places; Vercel paid plan or an
n8n timer for frequent jobs.

Phase 7 (built after Phase 9 at the owner's request): designers register at `/designers/register`,
staff approve them in Admin → Designers (after calling them), approved designers send projects
(measurements, chosen published décors, drawings and photos) and follow a simple status, and exchange
messages with staff. A submitted project becomes a normal lead and quotation request (source: designer
portal), so staff quote it in the existing flow. Not built: saving a project as a draft, adding files
after sending (use a message), designer email notifications (staff replies show in the portal; email
needs the owner's email account), password reset, and designers seeing quotation PDFs or prices.

Phase 10: everything that can be prepared without the owner's accounts: `/api/health` for uptime monitors, structured
server-error logs, `npm run launch:check` (go / no-go on the environment and database), nightly encrypted backup and
restore-rehearsal workflows with `npm run restore:verify`, CI with `npm audit`, `vercel.json` (scheduled job), an
untested `Dockerfile` for a host with Chrome, the staff guide ([ADMIN_GUIDE.md](ADMIN_GUIDE.md)), the technical
handover and runbook ([HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md)) and the list of what the owner must provide ([OWNER_INPUTS.md](OWNER_INPUTS.md)).
**Nothing has been deployed**: no domain, hosting, database, storage or email accounts exist yet, so production was never
verified and the backup workflows have never run.

Phase 8: AI assistants that only write drafts (off by default; they need a provider key on the server and the
owner's switch in Settings): a lead assistant (reply and summary drafts, shown without phone or email), a
content assistant (English and Arabic blog drafts) and an optional public furniture assistant at
`/furniture-assistant` (404 until switched on). Staff review, edit and approve each draft; approving only files
a note on the lead or creates an unpublished blog post. Automation: signed events to n8n (lead status,
quotations, designers, WhatsApp, enquiries with the customer's consent and an unsubscribe link), an
unsubscribe page, a signed inbound WhatsApp webhook that logs messages on the lead, example n8n workflows
in `docs/n8n`. Not built: any outbound email or WhatsApp to customers from automation (needs the owner's
accounts, templates and approval), automatic replies, voice or image features, AI price estimates.

Phase 9: independent security
review with all findings fixed or documented, Content-Security-Policy and COOP headers, per-address
limit on confirmation emails, upload bytes moved to a key the signed URL can't reach after checking,
sent quotation PDFs can't be replaced, sign-in lockout no longer lets a stranger lock staff out,
Lighthouse re-measured. Mobile performance is 81-86 (target 90); see TEST_REPORT.

Phase 6: quotations (draft, sent and locked, approved, declined, converted) with revisions, line items
priced by hand, BHD totals in integer fils, optional VAT (off until the owner enters a registration
number and rate), English or Arabic PDF rendered by Chrome and stored, approval recorded by phone or
WhatsApp with a note, conversion to an order and a project, project stages with history, and sales
figures on the dashboard. Not built: automatic price estimates (no rates are known, so none are
invented) and uploading a WhatsApp screenshot as proof of approval (the note is the record).

Phase 5: the admin portal at `/admin`: dashboard (real counts and a 12-week chart), leads (filters,
status, assignment, communication history, follow-ups), customers (edit, consent history),
consultations, notifications; website content (homepage hero, banner, SEO, materials library,
portfolio with photos, testimonials, blog, website images); settings (contact details, addresses,
hours, WhatsApp number); staff, roles and permissions; audit log. Public pages read the settings and
content from the database and fall back to the verified defaults when it can't be reached. The blog,
testimonials and décor library appear on the site only once staff publish real content.

Phase 4: database, staff sign-in with roles, and the quotation, consultation and contact forms saving
to it (with uploads, consent, outbox delivery and audit). A read-only enquiries view at `/admin` lets
staff see submissions until the Phase 5 CRM. Email sending needs the owner's notification addresses
and an email account (OPEN_QUESTIONS #41).

Phase 3: 21 of the 22 brief pages are live in English and Arabic, plus `/bespoke` and 21 project pages;
the blog waits for its first article (Phase 4). Quotation and consultation requests go by pre-filled email
and phone until the Phase 4 forms.

Phase 2 results: [TEST_REPORT.md](TEST_REPORT.md). Mobile Lighthouse performance (English 84–90,
Arabic 70–76) is below target and carried into Phase 9.
