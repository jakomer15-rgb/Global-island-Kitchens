## Conflicts to resolve

1. **Wall cladding material.** The profile says "Timber slat and panel cladding" (P3), and the brief says
   "wooden wall cladding". The materials rule says EGGER laminate/melamine and painted MDF only. The
   site will say "slatted and panel cladding" with the approved boards until the owner confirms.
2. **"All wood interior work" / "All wooden interior work"** (P7) reads as solid wood. Site copy will say
   "complete joinery" or "full interior joinery" unless the owner prefers otherwise.
3. **Appliances and imported products.** P2 says the company supplies appliances and accessories, locally
   made and imported. The brief describes a manufacturer. Which appliances, brands or accessories to show
   (if any) is unknown.
4. **Product list differences.** The profile has Murphy beds, laundry rooms, laminate doors and ceiling
   umbrellas; the brief does not. The brief's required pages include Wardrobes and Walk-in Closets as
   separate pages, and Bedroom Furniture. Proposed page mapping: see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#site-map).
5. **Ceiling umbrellas** are powder-coated (metal), outside the board materials rule. Confirm they stay
   in the product range.
