# Global Island Kitchens content rules

## Sources

The only sources of company information are `docs/BUSINESS_FACTS.md` (from the company profile PDF and
the owner's instructions). If a fact is not there, it is unknown: leave it out and add it to
`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. Never fill gaps with plausible-sounding details (years, awards, team names,
warranty terms, lead times, prices, review scores).

## Materials

- Boards are **EGGER laminate / melamine** and **painted MDF** only.
- Describe wood looks as "wood-grain", "oak-effect" or "walnut-effect" décors.
- Never write solid wood, natural timber, veneer or lacquer, and never name another board brand.
- EGGER may be named as the board brand. Never imply a partnership, authorisation or certification
  (no EGGER logo, no "official partner" wording).
- Painted MDF: say "painted MDF in the colour you choose" until sheens and colour system are confirmed.
- Aluminium and glass are used for aluminium kitchens and glass fronts.
- "Timber" or "all wood" wording in the company profile is not repeated on the site until the owner
  confirms the wording (see conflicts in `BUSINESS_FACTS.md`).

## Projects, photos, reviews

- Client and developer names appear only after the owner confirms permission for that project.
- Photos only with recorded usage permission. No stock photos presented as the company's work.
- No testimonials unless a real customer gave written consent (stored in the database).
- No prices or estimates unless staff have configured pricing rules.

## Voice

Calm, precise, confident; short sentences; British English spelling ("colour", "aluminium").
Arabic is written for Arabic readers (Modern Standard Arabic, natural phrasing), not translated word
for word; it is marked as a draft until a fluent reviewer approves it.
